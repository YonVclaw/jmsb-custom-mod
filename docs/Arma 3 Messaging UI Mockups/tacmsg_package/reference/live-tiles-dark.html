KICKER — app name, uppercase 9.5px/800 at 0.16em tracking, plus a right-aligned qualifier (net, unit, timestamp) in neutral-700.
VALUE — the one number or word worth a glance: Archivo 800 26px, tabular. Never two competing values.
DETAIL — one line at 1.35 leading, max two clauses separated by ·. Truncate rather than wrap to three lines.
FRAME — 176×98, 2px ink border, ground fill, zero radius, 9px/11px padding, 7px internal gap. Hover lifts one shadow step; press inverts to ink.
POLL — value refresh must not resize the tile: reserve the widest plausible value at build time.
1cTile → full-size app — tapping SQUAD opens over the map at full screen. The originating tile stays in place at the top-left of the header as the way back, and the reader zone on the right is preserved so comms never disappear.
