# Russian Uniforms

`jmfsb_uniform_ru`

The Russian army of the public Aegis sources (APL-SA), brought into jmfsb for 2040 Russia (2026-10-06): its soldiers -
woodland (taiga) and arid sets with their Aegis loadouts, all hidden (the faction classes on top are the units) - and the
kit they need that jmfsb did not already have: the taiga and arid combat and Luchnik uniforms, Luchnik helmet covers,
chest rigs, bags, the AK-12 / RPK-12 presets, the RPG-7M and the SR-1. The Gepard SMG stays out; its men carry the
AK-12U. The taiga and arid sheets are recoloured to e22 RAF's lesnoy and arid, and an arctic twin of the taiga kit is
made in its alpine greys (`arctic_*.hpp`).

    tools/aegis_port/port.py --seeds rus_seeds.txt      (in a sandbox copy)
    tools/aegis_port/port_to_addon.py <sandbox> uniform_ru
